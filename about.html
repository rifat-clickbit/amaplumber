Our story

Who we are

AmaPlumber Plumbing & Gas is a family-run business based north of Perth. We have built our name on honest, reliable service — every time.

Our services cover blocked drains, hot water installation, burst pipes and drainage. We perform every task to the best of our ability, with an incredible focus on our clients' requirements and an eye for the small details that make a difference.

We are known for being quick, friendly and reliable. We are available 24/7 for all your plumbing and gas needs, so please don't hesitate to give us a call.

Why locals call us first

From emergency call-outs at 5am to planned hot water upgrades, we treat every job as if it were at our own home. No shortcuts, no surprises, and no leaving a mess behind.
